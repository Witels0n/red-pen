How your files stay private

Most free online tools work by sending your file away to be processed somewhere else. Red Pen never does. Here is the difference.

A typical online tool

  1. You choose a file, and it is uploaded over the internet to the company's server.
  2. That server is a computer you cannot see, and it could be anywhere in the world. It opens your file and does the work.
  3. The result is sent back for you to download.
  4. A copy of your file is now on someone else's computer. You have to trust their promise about who can see it, how long it is kept, and that it never leaks.

Red Pen

  1. The first time you visit, your browser downloads the tool itself, the same way it downloads any web page.
  2. You choose a file, and your browser opens it right there on your own device.
  3. All the work happens on your computer, and the result is saved straight back to it.
  4. No server ever receives your file. Nothing was sent, so there is nothing for anyone to keep, leak or sell.
  1. Your files open in your browser. Every tool works on your own computer. Nothing is uploaded or stored on a server.
  2. The site blocks itself from sending data. Each page tells your browser to refuse any connection it tries to make, so this is enforced, not just promised.
  3. You can check this yourself. Once a page has loaded, turn off your Wi-Fi and use the tool. It still works. You're offline right now. See? Still works ✓

Install Red Pen

Install Red Pen like an app and it works fully offline, even when the school Wi-Fi is down.

Red Pen is installed on this device.

Your browser installs it from its own menu:

  • iPhone or iPad (Safari): tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android (Chrome): tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
  • Computer (Chrome or Edge): click the install icon at the right of the address bar.
