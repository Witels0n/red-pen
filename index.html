How your files stay private

  1. Your files open in your browser. Every tool works on your own computer. Nothing is uploaded or stored on a server.
  2. The site blocks itself from sending data. Each page tells your browser to refuse any connection it tries to make, so this is enforced, not just promised.
  3. You can check this yourself. Once a page has loaded, turn off your Wi-Fi and use the tool. It still works.

For school IT staff: the technical details are in the privacy decision record.

Install Red Pen

Install Red Pen like an app and it works fully offline, even when the school Wi-Fi is down.

Red Pen is installed on this device.

Your browser installs it from its own menu:

  • iPhone or iPad (Safari): tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android (Chrome): tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
  • Computer (Chrome or Edge): click the install icon at the right of the address bar.
