    How these are worked out
    • Every mark becomes a percentage of its "out of". Bands use the exact percentage before rounding, so 29.6% is in the 0-29 band. Figures are shown to one decimal place.
    • Absent learners (blank, ABS, A, AB, - or X) are counted but left out of the figures. Anything else that is not a mark is listed under its assessment for you to correct.
    • SD is the population standard deviation in percentage points: under 10 is a tight spread, 10 to 18 is typical and over 18 is wide. The quartiles split the marks into four equal groups; an outlier is more than 1.5 times the middle half's spread beyond it.
    • A skew is noted when the average and the median are 5 or more points apart. Two groups are looked for from 15 marks, as two peaks in 10% steps with few marks between. Over 30% in the top or bottom band is noted too. With fewer than 5 marks, these patterns are not shown.
    • Learners are matched across assessments by row: the names are only labels. Improved and dropped use only learners with both marks, and leave out calculated columns (Average, Total, Term mark).
    • The insights are prompts for your own judgement, not verdicts.
